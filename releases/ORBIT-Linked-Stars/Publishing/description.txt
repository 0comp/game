Turn one click into a cosmic energy empire in ORBIT! 🌍

Start on Earth, collect Energy, and build an empire across four connected star systems: the Sun, Proxima Centauri, Alpha Centauri A and Alpha Centauri B. Each star has 13 planets to develop.

⚡ Click planets to collect energy.
🛰️ Build 19 producer tiers, from collector drones to the mighty Oblivion engine.
🔥 Charge OVERDRIVE for a temporary x3 production boost.
🚀 Choose from 9 timed expeditions, including Pulsar relay and the Last-light voyage.
🔬 Research upgrades to grow faster.
🤖 Unlock Auto Buy in each system with 8 worlds and 500M Energy. It buys standard Research only and survives Ascend and discovery. After discovery, own 8 worlds again to use it.
☄️ Catch comet bonuses, earn 30 achievements, and complete 6 daily goals.
⭐ Ascend for stars and unlock local upgrades.
🌟 Complete all 13 planets around a star to discover the next one. Discovery resets ordinary progress in EVERY discovered system, including the Sun. You keep interstellar upgrades, Auto Buy ownership, achievement claims, daily progress and Robux benefits.
🔗 Buy permanent Interstellar upgrades in Research and Ascension to strengthen neighboring stars in both directions.
🗺️ Travel back to discovered systems without resetting their progress. Only your active system produces Energy.
💤 Return to collect offline earnings for your last active system.

Passes and paid boosts work across every star. Energy packs apply to the active star when delivered. The paid x2 production benefit is displayed in-game.

Play on PC, phone, or tablet. Click, tap, or press Space to collect energy.
