Start with one cookie and build a bakery empire in CRUMBLE, a 2D cookie clicker.

Click cookies, hire helpful bakers, buy ovens, and grow through eight bakery locations.

Charge SUGAR RUSH for a temporary x3 production boost.
Research upgrades and send your crew on timed deliveries.
Collect achievements, daily rewards, and golden crumbs.
Rebirth for stars and invest in permanent upgrades.
Return to collect capped offline production.

Play with a mouse, keyboard, or touch. Press Space or tap the cookie to bake.
