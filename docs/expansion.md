# Clicker expansion

Keep the experience entirely 2D and configurable. Replace the static presentation with responsive animation and add meaningful progression beyond the original three worlds/four upgrades.

## Deliverables

- Twelve producer tiers and eight worlds with increasing production and unlock requirements.
- Server-authoritative click charge: 20 accepted clicks trigger a six-second x3 boost. Research and permanent upgrades can extend it.
- A timed collectible with a short availability window and a server-calculated reward.
- Six research tracks purchased with energy; six permanent tracks purchased with rebirth stars.
- Three repeatable expeditions with different durations and rewards; one active expedition at a time.
- Sixteen one-time achievements and three daily objectives with explicit claims.
- Offline production capped by configurable capacity, with a return summary.
- Animated planet, stars, orbit, collectors, click impacts, currency interpolation, upgrade feedback and unlock celebrations. Optional reduced motion.
- Responsive Play, Research, Expeditions, Journal and Ascension screens; all content reachable on phones.
- Space and Bakery presets, source files, installation script and updated guide.
