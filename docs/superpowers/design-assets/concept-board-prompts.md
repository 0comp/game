## Original concept

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a polished landscape visual development board for our Roblox space exploration and pet-collecting game, working title STARFALL SALVAGERS. This is a new original concept using the attached images as style references, not a copy.
Reference image 1 is the desired world vocabulary: visible square studs, vivid grassy floating player plots, thick straight-cut rocky undersides, tan paths, brown chunky fences, angular green trees, crates and soft friendly light.
Reference image 2 is the desired pet vocabulary: a simple little angular toy creature with an expressive uncomplicated face, chunky body and small feet.
Reference images 3 and 4 guide interface: pixel lettering, studded bright header bars, dark panels, strong borders, colorful collectible cards and unknown silhouettes.
Composition: one large, beautifully composed oblique in-game-like view of the welcoming HOME SPACEPORT across the top two thirds. A thick grassy floating island against a deep blue-violet space sky with faint soft nebulae, small stars and recognizable distant planet silhouettes. Show a connected tan path and six neighboring fenced grassy player plots, with open sight lines between them. Each visible plot has a compact modular personal spaceship parked immediately beside a small group of adorable chunky space pets. Ships visibly differ in upgrades: a simple orange/turquoise starter craft, a craft with two larger block engines, and a sturdier cargo craft with stacked colorful eggs. Fixed attachment points and separate engines/cargo racks/shield modules read clearly as assembled toys. A small central plaza has a navigation device, a compact repair stand and cheerful robot shopkeepers. Add modest angular trees and little toy crates; leave generous walkable open space. A few classic blocky Roblox avatars supply scale and a cozy social atmosphere.
Bottom third: three clean, coordinated inset panels. Left: starter spaceship versus visibly upgraded spaceship, recognizable attachment points, green/turquoise/orange brick parts, no sleek realistic hulls. Middle: three ORIGINAL matching egg-and-pet pairs from a grassy starter world, turquoise crystal world, and strange violet alien world; each family shares simple shapes and colors, faces stay cute and extremely simple, cosmic accent is small orbiting fragments. Right: a clearly designed PET INDEX menu with a turquoise studded header, pixel title, dark charcoal-purple body, bright cards, a few black undiscovered silhouettes, consistent rarity outlines and a large green action button. UI is an illustration of style, not a functional application screenshot.
Art style: faithfully classic Roblox toy build with clearly visible SQUARE studs, chunky geometry and angular foliage. Flat plastic, crisp purposeful silhouettes, soft game lighting, saturated grass green, turquoise, orange, blue and purple. Use restraint: bright glow only on active engines, a rare egg and a shield. Thick floating islands, no thin levitating trays. Pleasant readable cheerful game-art presentation rather than a polished futuristic industrial station.
Text: only the board title "STARFALL SALVAGERS" and short section labels "HOME SPACEPORT", "BUILD YOUR SHIP", "EGGS & PETS", "PET INDEX". Keep lettering clean and legible.
Avoid photorealism, metal greebles, realistic sci-fi architecture, smooth potato ships, neon floors, purple grass at home, giant white hangars, dragons, copied fruit pets, giant UI over the world view, depth-of-field blur, game mechanic instructions, currency prices, drop-rate numbers, and watermarks.
